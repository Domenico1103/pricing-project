How do we estimate this value?

We compare with local office-hours monitoring at €5/device/month. The case assumes a 10% annual serious-incident probability and a 70% out-of-hours share. €4,800 reference fee + 10% × 70% × 95% × €140,000 incident saving + €900 insurance saving = €15,010.

Why one price for every sector?

The service and its device-based cost driver are the same. In the supplied survey, €12 gives the highest contribution in every sector among the four prices tested. Different sectors can receive different economic value without needing different prices or invented tiers.

Why pay per device?

Licences and analyst costs in the case are expressed per device. The same unit makes the bill easy to understand: enrolled devices × €12 per month. Monthly billing and cancellation, no minimum and no annual prepayment discount are proposed conditions.

Why a written commitment?

Of 40 lost deals, 35% doubted response speed and 20% cited price. A defined four-hour containment clock and capped service credit address response uncertainty directly. The commitment does not reimburse all losses or promise full recovery within four hours.

Why choose ShieldDesk?

Choose it if round-the-clock monitoring, analyst-led isolation and clear response terms fit your business. Local monitoring in the case costs €5/device/month and operates during office hours. Securalis charges €22/device/month with a 100-device minimum. ShieldDesk's proposed offer is €12 with no minimum. Compare coverage and terms, not price alone.

Your questions

Before you choose.

Does this replace our antivirus?

No. ShieldDesk adds round-the-clock monitoring and analyst response to the antivirus your business already uses.

Does four-hour containment mean full recovery?

No. Containment means isolating the affected device. Analysts begin recovery, but the commitment does not specify a deadline for full restoration.

What happens if the containment deadline is missed?

An eligible miss earns one monthly service bill as a credit, capped at one credit per customer per year. The enrolled device must be connected and accessible with the necessary isolation permissions.

How is my monthly price calculated?

Multiply your enrolled device count by €12. Prices exclude VAT. Billing is monthly, with monthly cancellation, no minimum device count and no annual prepayment discount.
