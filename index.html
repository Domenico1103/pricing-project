Your questions

Before you choose.

Does this replace our antivirus?

No. ShieldDesk adds round-the-clock monitoring and analyst response to the antivirus your business already uses.

Does four-hour containment mean full recovery?

No. Containment means isolating the affected device. Analysts begin recovery, but the commitment does not specify a deadline for full restoration.

What happens if the containment deadline is missed?

An eligible miss earns one monthly service bill as a credit, capped at one credit per customer per year. The enrolled device must be connected and accessible with the necessary isolation permissions.

How is my monthly price calculated?

Multiply your enrolled device count by €12. Prices exclude VAT. Billing is monthly, with monthly cancellation, no minimum device count and no annual prepayment discount.
